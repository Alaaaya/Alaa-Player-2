Lulu is an additive selectable theme, stored as `lulu`. All prior themes remain intact.

The floating teal navigation capsule replaces Ivano's full-height rail. Home uses a rotating hero, poster carousels, continue-watching progress and a breathing teal backdrop. Posters use a white focus outline and gold play marker. Movie and series details have gold pill actions; series keep season selection and episode resume wiring. Live TV retains channel preview, favorites, category selection and long-press channel actions. The player uses the glass control bar with gold transport and retains the shared playback callbacks and seek support. Search retains real query/index callbacks and the remote keyboard. Settings use a compact navigation column and a glass content panel.

Existing proven bespoke components were adapted for Lulu, keeping provider and player business logic unchanged. This is not a new playback engine.

The real Combined M3U profile picker in settings has a Lulu-specific avatar-card hook with selected and active indicators. It uses real profile selection callbacks and preserves creation, activation and membership editing. These are playlist/source profiles, not invented household accounts. No emulator is connected, so visual/D-pad behavior on hardware still needs testing. Build results are recorded separately; compilation is not visual validation.
